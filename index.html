About

A multidisciplinary engineer with system-level depth.

I combine electronic hardware, embedded firmware, FPGA acceleration, machine learning, system integration, and verification to develop reliable solutions for technically demanding applications.

Professional profile

My experience includes analog, digital, and mixed-signal circuit design; Altium schematic and PCB design; LTspice simulation; board bring-up; troubleshooting; root-cause analysis; worst-case analysis; and FMEA.

I have developed systems around STM32, TMS and PIC microcontrollers, AMD/Xilinx FPGAs and SoCs, Qualcomm, Raspberry Pi and NVIDIA Jetson platforms, with interfaces including MIPI, PCIe, SDI, Ethernet, CAN, SPI, I²C, UART, RS-232 and RS-485.

Engineering approach

I focus on requirements-driven architecture, practical implementation, measurable validation, and clear technical documentation. My background includes power trees, sequencing, protection, sensor acquisition, image/video processing, high-speed links, embedded Linux, FreeRTOS, and FPGA-based hardware acceleration.

Selected Projects

Products and engineering projects.

PROJECT 01 · SPACE ELECTRONICS

Multi-Spectral Camera Calibration Controller for a Class-2 Satellite

Hardware controller for calibrating a multi-spectral satellite camera, developed with consideration of ESCC and space-component design practices.

  • Requirements analysis and hardware architecture definition.
  • Current and voltage telemetry for an isolated 40 W heater load.
  • Complete power-management block: DC/DC, LDO, UVLO, OVLO, sequencing, monitoring, protection, and EMI filtering.
  • High-accuracy RTD temperature measurement with 0.05 K tolerance and online calibration.
  • Pin-puller and dual stepper-motor actuator control, FMEA, worst-case analysis, and full schematic design.
Project image
PROJECT 02 · AUTONOMOUS SYSTEMS

Distributed Multi-Sensor Acquisition and Processing Platform

Low-latency, high-throughput platform for autonomous ground and aerial vehicles, designed around distributed sensing, FPGA acceleration, precise timing, and heterogeneous processing.

  • Hardware/software architecture aligned with military and NATO requirements.
  • Sensor board with dual MIPI camera inputs, SDI input, HDMI output, and Artix UltraScale+ FPGA.
  • 10G PHY, IEEE 1588v2 PTP timestamping, SyncE, and clock-jitter attenuation.
  • Redundant power input, power multiplexer, conversion rails, and voltage/current monitoring.
  • Processing path from multiple 10G inputs to Zynq UltraScale+, PCIe, and Qualcomm GPU/NPU/encoder processing.

PROJECT 03 · BCI WEARABLE

Intelligent Headphone Based on Brain-Computer Interface

Miniaturized wearable platform that combined EEG acquisition, acoustic beamforming, inertial sensing, edge ML, and real-time embedded control.

  • 10–16-channel low-amplitude EEG acquisition and preprocessing.
  • MEMS microphone acquisition and direction-of-arrival beamforming.
  • Neural-network inference for blink and eye-angle detection on the edge.
  • IMU/compass calibration, FreeRTOS task scheduling, battery charging, and power monitoring.

PROJECT 04 · FPGA VIDEO PROCESSING

Real-Time Embedded Video Processing System

End-to-end heterogeneous imaging platform based on a Xilinx Zynq-7000 SoC for concurrent infrared and RGB video acquisition and low-latency processing.

  • Concurrent HD infrared and Full-HD RGB acquisition in FPGA logic.
  • VDMA-based shared-memory video transfer and ARM-side low-level drivers.
  • Custom HLS/VHDL IP for resizing, filtering, overlay, and motion detection.
  • HDMI output, complete schematic and PCB design, remote ground-station control, and field reprogramming.
  • Approximately 5× reduction in size/weight, 10× lower power, and 3× lower latency.

Research

Research direction and publications.

My doctoral research investigates automatic optimization of applications running on heterogeneous FPGA-based computing platforms using machine learning.

Current doctoral research

The research focuses on graph-based representations, machine-learning prediction of FPGA quality-of-results, multi-fidelity learning, and design-space exploration for high-level synthesis. The objective is to reduce the cost of design exploration while accurately predicting resource usage, timing, and latency across heterogeneous FPGA implementations.
